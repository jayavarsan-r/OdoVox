## Designer's note

The flag is on the drug, not in a banner — a Persian-red rail + red drug name + one factual line (what the allergy is, when recorded), the way a chart annotation reads. No pink washes, no AI voice. Findings/procedure left the bento: a paper block — hairline rule, 16.5 px/1.7, canvas background — reads like a clinical note. Taps: patient name → record · next-sitting tile → that appointment · Swap → resolves in place · red line → full allergy history · 21 mm · verify → cursor lands on the number · pen → free edit · Confirm locked until the rail clears.
