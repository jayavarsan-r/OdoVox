## Designer's note

The habit hook is the day river — 8 segments, one per appointment: lime = done, dark = in the chair now, hollow = ahead. One glance = where am I in my day; filling it is the daily loop. Stat pills are gone (their numbers live in the caption line). Every tap: river/caption → Schedule · name (has chevron) → patient record · red allergy text → medical flags · CTA/orb → Recording · Lab tile → Lab (red count = overdue) · Schedule tile → day view · Day-off bar → block sheet · ＋ → speed dial · queue card → patient · "View all" → Follow-ups · message row → thread.
