## Designer's note

Low-confidence tokens get the dotted warn underline — never silently guessed. Three repair paths, all in place: tap a candidate chip, re-say it (voice correction re-parses just that field), or tap to type. Spell-it captures letter by letter. Create stays locked only while a flagged field is unresolved; everything already-parsed survives the fix.
